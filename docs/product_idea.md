# Hapkonic Boutique - Product Idea & Documentation

## Vision
A premium, immersive digital showcase for exquisite Indian jewelry, designed to bridge heritage craftsmanship with modern digital elegance. The Boutique-demo-website is an "Atelier" for the digital age, focusing on visual brilliance, storytelling, and a seamless luxury shopping experience.

## Target Audience
- **Luxury Shoppers**: Individuals seeking high-end, curated jewelry with a focus on quality and heritage.
- **Bridal segment**: Couples looking for premium solitaire rings and bridal sets.
- **Design Enthusiasts**: People who appreciate the intersection of traditional Indian designs and modern presentation.

## Key Features

### 1. Immersive Visual Experience
- **3D Floating Showcase**: A dynamic, interactive display of hero products (e.g., diamonds) to emphasize brilliance and detail.
- **Scroll Reveals & Micro-animations**: Using custom hooks to reveal content smoothly as the user scrolls, creating a premium feel.
- **Custom Cursor & Grainy Overlay**: Aesthetic refinements that give the site a tactile, high-fashion magazine aesthetic.

### 2. Digital Catalogue ("The Book")
- **Interactive Product Books**: Products are presented as "Volumes" or "Collections" that users can interact with.
- **Curated Collections**:
    - **Eternity (Coll. 01)**: Focused on Solitaire Rings and Bridal pieces.
    - **Celestial (Coll. 02)**: Signature 18k gold pendants with sapphire accents.
    - **Royal Drop (Coll. 03)**: Couture emerald sets with micro-pave diamonds.

### 3. "Atelier" Storytelling
- A dedicated section (Atelier) that focuses on the craftsmanship and heritage behind the pieces, moving beyond just commerce into branding.

### 4. Seamless eCommerce Flow
- **Floating Cart**: A minimalist, non-intrusive cart system for easy management of selected items.
- **Success Experience**: A premium modal feedback system for order placement.

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React (v19)
- **Tooling**: Vite (for rapid development and optimized builds)
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS (focused on high-performance animations and custom layouts)
- **Architecture**: Component-based architecture with custom hooks (`useCustomCursor`, `useScrollReveal`) for shared UI logic.

## Design Philosophy
The design follows a "Luxe Interactive" approach:
- **Typography**: Playful yet elegant font choices (e.g., italicized emphasis).
- **Color Palette**: Dark mode by default with glowing accents and high-quality photography.
- **Layout**: Non-traditional, grid-breaking layouts that feel like a luxury lookbook.
